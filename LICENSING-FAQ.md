# U9GL Licensing FAQ — Version 1.0

This FAQ explains the licence; `LICENSE.md` controls if they differ. U9GL is **source-available**, not OSI-approved open source, because the licence restricts organisational and commercial activity.

## Can I use U9GL at home with Ultima IX?
Yes, for your personal hobby use, free of charge. This does not provide a copy or licence for Ultima IX itself.

## Can I modify or build U9GL for myself?
Yes. You don't need to publish private modifications you don't distribute.

## Can I fork the repository on GitHub?
Yes, for personal non-commercial purposes. Preserve copyright and licence notices. A fork that distributes a modified version must include its corresponding source and modification notices. A GitHub fork does not magically acquire a commercial licence.

## Can I publish a modded U9GL binary?
Yes, for personal non-commercial distribution, but include the licence, identify changes and make the complete corresponding source for that binary available to recipients, including build scripts. You may distribute source alongside it or provide the durable download location specified by the licence.

## Must I put my changes on a public GitHub repository?
No. The rule is **source to recipients when copies are distributed**, not public posting of every private modification. A public download does mean source must be available to its recipients.

## If I write a separate tool that communicates with U9GL, does it become covered?
Not automatically. Merely using an ordinary interface does not necessarily create a derivative work. If your work copies or incorporates protected portions of U9GL, the licence may apply. Borderline cases require assessment.

## Can a company use U9GL internally without distributing it?
**Not under the free licence.** Companies and other organisations must first obtain a paid written commercial licence, even for internal use, builds, tests or integrations. The same applies to employees or contractors acting on their behalf.

## Does the company need to publish internal source changes?
Not merely for internal use. A company must obtain a paid commercial licence, but source-sharing applies when it distributes a covered work, subject to its agreement.

## What about charities, government organisations, universities, or clubs?
Under this policy, organisational use requires a separate licence. The copyright holder may offer discretionary discounts or a no-fee written organisational licence; there is **no automatic exemption**.

## What if I am an individual earning money on the side?
Business, paid-contract or primarily revenue-generating use requires commercial permission. Purely voluntary donations unrelated to access, services or deliverables do not by themselves change the nature of hobby use. Paid access, paid support, sponsorship deliverables, ad-supported distribution and employer-sponsored work should be discussed with the licensor.

## Can a corporation pay once and keep its changes closed source?
Paying does **not** automatically waive the source-sharing requirement on distribution. Private internal modifications can remain private. Any exceptional closed-source redistribution rights would require a **separately negotiated, express written exception**; these are not part of the standard policy.

## Can a company sell copies of U9GL?
Only if its written commercial licence authorises that distribution, and it follows the source-sharing conditions. Buying a copy from somebody else does not grant commercial permission.

## Can I contribute a bug fix or feature?
Yes, after the maintainer accepts a signed contributor licence agreement (CLA). You retain ownership but grant the U9GL licensor broad rights, including the right to commercial-license your contribution. Do not send third-party code without the necessary permissions.

## Do I need my employer's permission to contribute?
If your employer owns or may claim rights in the contribution, you must obtain written authorisation or the employer must sign the appropriate agreement. Do not assume personal GitHub activity means you own the code.

## Is U9GL GPLv3 or PolyForm Noncommercial?
No. It is a custom licence with similar copyleft mechanics, **not** GPLv3, PolyForm, or an OSI-approved open-source licence. You cannot assume GPL-compatible mixing of code.

## Does U9GL grant permission to use Ultima IX game files, assets or trademarks?
No. The U9GL licence only covers rights the U9GL licensor owns or is allowed to license.

## How do I request a commercial licence?
Contact the licensing address in [README.md, "Licence"](README.md#licence) with the organisation's legal name, use case, installations, redistribution plans and desired support terms. Fees and terms must be agreed in writing before business use begins.

## What if I find a licence violation?
Please contact the licensing address in [README.md, "Licence"](README.md#licence) with the affected release or repository and supporting details. Do not publish personal information or make allegations without evidence.
