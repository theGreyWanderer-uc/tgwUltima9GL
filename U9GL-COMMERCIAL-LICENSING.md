Under the U9GL Non-Commercial Copyleft Licence 1.0, **companies and other organisations do not receive free-use permission**. A signed, paid commercial agreement is required before use by or for an organisation, including internal testing, development, integration and deployment. Sole traders and contractors working in the course of business also need permission.

## Standard commercial policy

- Licence fees and scope are negotiated **before** commercial use; there is no automatic permission based on mere receipt of source code.
- Commercial distribution of U9GL or a Covered Work is allowed only if the signed agreement authorises it.
- **Source-sharing on distribution remains mandatory** under the standard paid licence: recipients must be supplied complete corresponding source and applicable notices.
- Internal modifications need not be published merely because they exist.
- Closed-source redistribution is **not** included in the standard paid licence; any exception must be expressly negotiated and signed. The licensor may decline requests.
- Support, maintenance, duration, renewal, installations, entities covered, permitted distribution channels, payment, termination, liability and applicable taxes must be set out in the signed agreement.

This policy is not itself a commercial licence, price quotation, legal permission or binding offer. A tailored contract must be signed before commercial use.
